## Location and parking
We are at 1450 SW 8th St (Calle Ocho), Miami, FL 33135, in Little Havana. Free street parking on the side streets; metered parking on Calle Ocho.

## Walk-ins
Walk-ins are welcome when a barber is free, but appointments get priority. Saturdays are busy, so booking ahead is recommended.

## Payment
We accept cash, all major credit cards, Apple Pay and Zelle. Tips can be added on card.

## Cancellations and late arrivals
Please cancel or reschedule at least 2 hours before your appointment. If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule.

## Kids
Kids haircut is for children under 12. A parent or guardian must stay in the shop.

## Products
We sell pomade, beard oil and aftershave in the shop. We do not ship products.

## Languages
Our barbers speak English and Spanish.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available in the shop in any amount. They are not sold online.

## Services we do not offer
We do not do hair coloring, perms, braids, or women's long-hair styling.
